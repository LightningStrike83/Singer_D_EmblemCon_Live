DENNY'S SYLVAIN EVENT

As a special Day of Devotion side event, Lord Sylvain and Great Lord Mysti held a livestream from a Denny's, based on an inside joke from the previous year's Gartic Phone panel. There, Lord Sylvain cosplayed as Sylvain and rated ships sent in by donators while Mysti drew a little doodle of them.

Thanks to their efforts, EmblemCon was able to raise $127 for The Trevor Project and Great Lord Mysti and Lord Sylvain doodled and rated over 12 ships!
