ABOUT THE CONVENTION

Mascot Art by Faiwonton

EmblemCon is a free digital Discord convention made by Fire Emblem fans, for Fire Emblem fans. Please have a look around each section to find out more about our events and guests! Feel free to check out our FAQ for more information as well!

▼ See A Trailer For The Convention ▼

GET STARTED

Wondering where to start with the EmblemCon experience? Here's some suggestions on where to start!

  • • Visit our Registration page to see how to join the convention and how to connect with other fans of the Fire Emblem franchise!
  • • Keep an eye on our Applications page to see how you can become a part of the EmblemCon experience; whether you're an artist or a panelist, we'd love to host you!
  • • Want some fun and chaotic content before the convention? Listen to our podcast on our Paralogues page or on our Youtube channel!
