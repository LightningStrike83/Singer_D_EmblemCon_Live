REGISTRATION

EmblemCon is a free convention hosted over Discord yearly. Please find instructions on how to access the event down below. All instructions apply to desktop, desktop application, and mobile users.

We invite you to stick around year-round where we have lovely discussion with our off-season community, host streams on our Twitch, and host an occassional mini-event every now and then.

STEP 1

Click the Discord icon in our header or hamburger menu, or click this link to get an invitation to the con. This can be done at any time throughout the year.

STEP 2

Once the invite has been accepted and you have entered the server, please read the rules to ensure everyone enjoys the convention.

STEP 3

When the convention is active, please find the channel "role-assignments" under the "Important" category of our channel list.

STEP 4

In the "role-assignments", find the message posted from our of our mods that says to "React to give yourself a badge." and react with the designated icon to receive a role that will grant access to all the event-related channels.

(Please allow a moment for the channels to load)

STEP 5

Enjoy yourself!
