APPLICATIONS

Looking to become part of EmblemCon? We'd love to have you! Please check out the information of each area down below, and submit your application. Please refer to our FAQ if you have any questions on the process.

APPLICANT GUIDE

At EmblemCon, we are dedicated in providing a safe space for everyone. As such, we highly request that you please review our guide before applying. This guide provides all the information in what EmblemCon is looking for in applicants. Thank you!

Applicant Guide can be found here

ARTIST ALLEY

Our Artist Alley hosts a variety of artists of various skill levels to come and showcase their Fire Emblem related art & merchandise and to hang out with other artists and fans. Rosado would think everyone's art is super cute!

Applications are currently open

EXHIBITORS HALL

Our exhibitors hall invites those with Fire Emblem-themed merchandise and services to come and share their wares. Some you may not even find at Anna's Secret Shop!

Applications are currently open

CREATIVES CORRIDOR

In our Creatives Corridor, you'll find a variety of artists who are willing to offer their services to help bring your Fire Emblem-themed ideas to life. Any artist who may not have Fire Emblem-related merchandise is more than welcome to apply. This is where Flayn would ask to be "illustrious".

Applications are currently open

PANELIST

Have a panel idea you'd like to share with the fanbase? We'd love to hear it! We accept a variety of panels, from cosplay Q&As to game shows. Just make sure you don't bury anyone in flower petals like Cynthia.

Applications are currently open
