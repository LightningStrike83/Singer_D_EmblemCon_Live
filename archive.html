EmblemCon Halloween 2025- Escape The Castle

In 2025, Darkening (Mod Lightning's evil counterpart) hosted a panel featuring Anni, Ciarre, and Okke as part of a special Halloween event. Participants had to answer special questions and solve puzzles to unlock the ending. And there was a huge twist at end which shocked everyone!
