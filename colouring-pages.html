COLOURING PAGES

Since EmblemCon 2022, Anni / Lorebird has made special colouring pages that she graciously shares with the attendees! You can find an archive of these pages down below, where she's allowed EmblemCon to host them year-round so fans can enjoy them at any time!

You can download the whole package, or you can download each one individually by clicking on a page and clicking the associated download button! You can also colour these digitally or you can print them out and colour them with your own art tools!
