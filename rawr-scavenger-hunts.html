SUPER SECRET SCAVENGER HUNT

As an extra and fun bonus for EmblemCon, Lord Rawr led a scavenger hunt during EmblemCon 2024. The inspiration for this came from PAX, where there was an activity that would have you scan QR Codes that were found throughout different areas of the convention. Rawr led development of a bot that would provide the same experience, but only be visible to users so that way it wouldn't be intrusive to the convention and the artists.

Going to a channel in the artist alley and typing in a command would pop up a private message from the bot, which displayed an item from that artist's booth and an egg that was associated with your booth. As you visited more booths, the egg would level up.

After doing this at every booth, the egg would reach its final level and you were rewarded with a link to a private Discord to celebrate with other EmblemCon attendees, as well as a special "Egg Wrangler" role for EmblemCon.
