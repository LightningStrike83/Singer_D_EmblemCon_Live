STAMP RALLY

Stamp Rallies are where attendees can fulfill certain requirements from designated artists in the Artist Alley to acquire a stamp. Once all of the stamps have been collected from all of the artists, the attendee can receive special rewards from those artists!

To view an archive of past stamp rallies, please visit this page.
