THE EMBLEMCON EXPERIENCE

Wondering what there is to do at EmblemCon? Or what it is that makes this convention so special? Look no further! Check out just a fraction of what EmblemCon has to offer!

Panels

Whether it's run by our lovely guests, or by our amazing con-goers, EmblemCon has been host to a large variety of Fire Emblem themed panels that can suit almost everyone's tastes! In the past, we've had panels such as:
• Q & A with Fire Emblem voice actors and content creators
• Cutest of the Con, hosted by Brian Timothy Anderson
• A variety of Fire Emblem themed game shows, including Wheel of Fortune, Family Feud, and Jeopardy-inspired games
• Live playthroughs and dubbings of Fire Emblem games
• Q & A with cosplayers in character

Cosplay

Whether you're new to cosplay or have been doing it for a while, you're more than welcome to come and share your cosplay with everyone! We try and provide a safe space for everyone to share and ask questions if they ever need!

Creatives

EmblemCon has many spaces for creatives to share their work! Whether that's by tabling at our Artist Alley & Creatives Corridor & Exhibitors Hall, attending our art-inspired panels, or even just sharing in our related channels, we welcome you to share the fruits of your creativity, as long as it's within our rules and guidelines!

Discussions

EmblemCon is home to many to share their thoughts and discussions with others. Whether you want to discuss the games in our related channels during the con, engage others by hosting a panel, or even just participating in our off-season general chat, we have many lovely people from all over the globe to share with. And, of course, all of this is moderated to ensure everyone's safety!

Memes

Throughout the years, EmblemCon has had some memorable experiences that have turned into inside jokes amongst our con-goers and special guests. Some of these include, but aren't limited to:
• Maraschino Cherries on Pizza
• #StanJennyYokobori
• #AllegraCon
